# Naming exploration

Status: name and logo direction selected, 2026-09-20. **The user chose Elsebase** and then selected the first logo proposal, **The doorway beyond (Die Tür ins Anderswo)**. The user also likes hearing an association with “El-space”; this is a creative sound association, not a claim of identical pronunciation. Production logo assets remain unfinished. After subsequent implementation authorization, the namespace/package are now elsebase / dev.elsebase; see implementation.md.

## Brief and method

Choose a memorable, noticeable name that hints at a permanent base in another dimension and fits technology, magic and optional dark survival styles. The user proposed Otherspace and Elsebase. The user is the decision owner. Current gameplay is still a design, not a released product.

Methods used: `research-brand-market-audience` for product/category truth and collision screening; `develop-brand-strategy` for benefit and claim boundaries. The earlier `build-brand-identity` / `imagegen` exploration was premature and is not an adopted identity. That pending image revision was terminated at the user's correction. After the name selection, three new Elsebase proposals were generated and saved as discussion assets; no logo is installed as the mod icon.

The marketing runtime's read-only inspection reports missing `.marketing-os/client.json`. These remain reversible discussion drafts in the software project's authorized `docs/` workflow, without marketing-workspace adoption or any strategy/identity activation.

## Shortlist considered before selection

| Name | Meaning and strength | Trade-off | Preliminary search finding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Elsebase** | A base somewhere else. Compact, straightforward, and directly connected to the intended use rather than a particular visual theme. | Hints less directly at the portal itself; the product description should explain access. | No exact named product or Minecraft mod surfaced in the tested exact/qualified searches. This is not availability proof. |
| **Otherspace** | Another space beyond ordinary geography. Smooth, broad and suitable for technical or magical presentations. | Less specific to base building, and existing entertainment uses weaken search distinctiveness. | Existing FTL mod and other unrelated uses observed; see sources below. |
| **Elsehold** | A place or stronghold elsewhere. Shorter fantasy-leaning identity for a lasting building location. | The English word “hold” is less immediately understood than “base”; can overemphasize castles or safety unless presented carefully. | No exact named gaming product surfaced in the tested query; irrelevant text fragments were returned. |

The creative recommendation was **Elsebase**, balancing concept clarity, simple pronunciation and flexible appearance. The user subsequently selected it. Otherspace and Elsehold are now historical alternatives. These creative judgments are not measured recognition results; selection does not mean a platform name has been reserved or legal availability established.

## Product and naming boundaries

Confirmed design intent: a shared permanent dimension, personal reference points, recallable entrances, editable room structure, seven interchangeable styles and optional darkness/mob spawning. The base stays where it is; the doorway can move. Do not imply transported machines, a private dimension per player, unconditional safety, never-unloading factories or live portal views in the initial test version.

Use international English with straightforward spelling. An explanatory subtitle can carry the mechanism; the main name should not attempt to list every feature. No commercial forecast, audience-size claim or launch date is supported or needed for this choice.
